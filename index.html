Day 1 · Theme

Why Meaning Matters

Throughout history, people have searched for answers to the most profound questions about life, purpose, suffering, and happiness. Religions serve as sacred guides through these mysteries — offering spiritual teachings, moral frameworks, and a deep sense of hope and belonging.


This theme is essential today because many people struggle with stress, loneliness, and existential uncertainty. By exploring how different faiths understand meaning and purpose, we open pathways of mutual understanding and profound personal reflection.


Every religion attempts to answer universal human questions — even when their teachings differ, they share the pursuit of compassion, peace, and spiritual growth.

☮
"Exploring how different religions understand meaning allows people to learn from one another and reflect on their own beliefs and values."

Day 1 · Sacred Words

Scripture Passages

Three traditions. One universal longing. Explore how Christianity, Buddhism, and Islam each illuminate the search for meaning.

Christianity
✝
"For I know the plans I have for you," declares the Lord, "Plans to prosper you and not to harm you, plans to give you hope and a future."
Jeremiah 29:11 · Holy Bible
This scripture teaches that every person has purpose and direction in life. In Christianity, meaning is found through faith in God and trust in His divine plan — offering hope during times of uncertainty and reminding believers that life holds value even through struggle.
Buddhism
☸
"Peace comes from within. Do not seek it without."
The Dhammapada · Buddhist Scripture
This teaching explains that fulfillment and peace are found through self-awareness and inner growth rather than material possessions or outside validation. Buddhism shows that understanding oneself and overcoming suffering leads to enlightenment and a life of genuine meaning.
Islam
☪
"And whoever puts their trust in Allah, then He alone is sufficient for them."
Qur'an 65:3 · The Holy Quran
Islam teaches that true meaning comes through faith, trust, and devotion to Allah. Muslims believe life holds purpose when people follow God's guidance and live morally and compassionately — remaining hopeful and faithful even through life's greatest challenges.
Day 2 · Workshop

Inter-Religious Dialogue

Four questions to open hearts and minds — designed to spark respectful, meaningful conversation between people of all faith traditions.

01
How do different religions help people find meaning and purpose during difficult times in life?
02
Do you believe people can truly find meaning without spirituality or religion? Why or why not?
03
The Christian, Buddhist, and Islamic scriptures each describe different paths toward peace and fulfillment. Which ideas are most relevant to young people today?
04
How can understanding another religion's beliefs about purpose and meaning help reduce stereotypes and conflict between people?

Day 3 · Panel

Speaker Panel

Three voices. Three faiths. One shared commitment to compassion, peace, and the flourishing of humanity.

✝
Pope Francis
Christianity · Rome

A global voice for compassion, humility, and interfaith cooperation. His teachings on human dignity and care for the poor connect deeply to the search for meaning through service.

  • Human Dignity — Every person holds inherent value regardless of religion, race, or status
  • Peace & Justice — Ending violence begins with listening and mutual respect
  • Care for the Poor — Helping the vulnerable gives deeper meaning to human life
  • Environmental Responsibility — Creation is a gift from God, to be protected for all
☸
The Dalai Lama
Buddhism · Tibet

A living symbol of inner peace and non-violence, teaching that genuine happiness arises from compassion and self-understanding — not from wealth or achievement.

  • Compassion — Caring for others creates stronger communities and meaningful lives
  • Inner Peace — True peace comes from within through mindfulness and growth
  • Nonviolence — Patience and understanding build harmony; anger creates suffering
  • Self-Reflection — Understanding oneself is the path to wisdom and fulfillment
☪
Malala Yousafzai
Islam · Pakistan

A beacon of courage and Islamic values in action — using faith as the foundation for advocating education, equality, and human rights for young people worldwide.

  • Education & Knowledge — Islam values learning for all people, men and women alike
  • Courage & Perseverance — Standing for justice even in fear demonstrates true faith
  • Equality & Human Rights — Every person deserves dignity, freedom, and opportunity
  • Hope & Purpose — Belief and determination can inspire profound change in the world
Day 4 · Workshop

Finding Meaning Through Prayer & Reflection

A sacred space for participants of all faiths to experience moments of peace, reflection, and spiritual connection — together yet individually.

Workshop Schedule
5 min
Introduction
Welcome participants. Explain interfaith prayer and respectful reflection. Introduce the theme: "The Search for Meaning."
5 min
Silent Reflection
Soft instrumental music plays. Participants reflect on what gives meaning and purpose to their lives.
5 min
Christian Prayer Reading
A short Bible reading about hope and purpose is shared. Participants reflect quietly on Jeremiah 29:11.
10 min
Buddhist Meditation
Guided breathing and mindfulness meditation, focused on inner peace and self-awareness.
5 min
Islamic Prayer Reflection
Qur'an 65:3 is read aloud. Participants reflect on perseverance, trust, and hope.
10 min
Group Reflection Discussion
What ideas connected across the prayers? How did different practices feel? What was learned?
5 min
Closing Reflection
A final moment of silence. Participants are encouraged to continue exploring different faiths respectfully.

Sacred Iconography

Symbols of Faith

Each symbol carries centuries of meaning — chosen to represent the hopes, values, and spiritual journey at the heart of this conference.

✝
The Christian Cross
Symbolizes hope, sacrifice, love, and faith in God. One of the most sacred symbols in Christianity, representing the teachings and sacrifice of Jesus Christ. Placed on a small table near the front of the prayer room beside a candle — connecting directly to the conference theme of searching for meaning and purpose.
🪷
The Lotus Flower
In Buddhism, the lotus represents purity, spiritual awakening, and inner peace. It symbolizes growth — rising from muddy water to bloom in beauty. Placed at the center of the prayer room, connecting to the idea of personal growth and finding peace through self-reflection and wisdom.
☪
The Star & Crescent
A widely recognized symbol of Islam, representing faith, divine guidance, and the light of truth. It connects to the Islamic teaching that life holds meaning through trust in Allah and righteous living — a reminder to remain hopeful and faithful through all of life's challenges.
☮
The Peace Symbol
Transcending any single tradition, the peace symbol embodies the shared aspiration across all faiths — that compassion, understanding, and respectful dialogue can heal divisions and create a world where every human being can find meaning, belonging, and hope.
